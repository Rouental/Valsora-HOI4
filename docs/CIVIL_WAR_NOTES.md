# The Rouentaise civil war: the author's plans

Notes for when the civil war is picked up again (the author, 2026-10-02). What is built
now is in `CLAUDE.md` (Rouental's focus tree); this is what it should become.

## Built so far

- **Kick Out the Parasites (Rudely):** Rouental turns communist, moves its capital to
  Carcarelle and is cast out of the Association of Reibonnaise States, which re-forms
  under Hollier. Its communist flag is the Liberté flag.
- **Execute the Prince:** the superevent plays and Rouental becomes the Reibonne.
  Lustiana takes Maïeul and Serpette, Hollier takes Chirac, and the Association becomes
  the Alliance of the Vale: a credible threat on the player's border. For now, every
  revolting faction appears at once and declares war on the Reibonne.

## To come

- The revolters spawn **by event after the focus**, not all at once.
- **HMMLA appears first**; the longer the war lasts, the more tags appear.
- At least two kinds of revolter, in rival factions, **all at war with all**:
  - **Unifiers**, in a faction led by HMMLA, who want to reunify Rouental.
  - **Disunifiers**, in a faction led by Crépuscule, who want Rouental broken up.
  - Perhaps a third: faith-based unifiers.
- Which existing tag belongs to which side is still open.
