# Rouental's fiefs on the game's states

Made 2026-10-02 by overlaying the author's fief map (`Rouental_Map_TEMP_2.pdn`) on the game's
states (`dist/rouental_fiefs_overlay.png`). Each fief label was read, matched to
`source/names/rouental_fiefs.txt` and placed in the state under most of its area. The data is
`source/rouental_fiefs_by_state.json` (state → its fiefs, by cultural group).

**Every fief is a vassal of the Crown** (author, 2026-10-02). The 14 headings of the fief list
(Carcasonnaise, Conflans, Northmarch…) are cultural groups, not vassals; the governorates
above the vassals have no power. The colours on the overlay are those cultural groups.
Each fief's title is still to come from the author.

## Not placed

- States with no fief on the map: Moelle (north of Maïeul, so probably Lustianaise), Concorde et
  Volonté (the islets off Solitas), Brillagne (the island; its label isn't in the fief list).
  Rêverie is set to the Royal Fiefs.
- Fiefs just across the border by the overlay (it is ±3 game px at the edges): Seignon and
  Coffrefort (Northmarch) on Cardonia's side, Merrac (Southmarch) on Placeholdros' side; Corseque
  (Lustianaise) in Lustiana and Scheldt, Domän (Oscarl) in Evriches. They are left out of the file.
- Labels on the map that aren't in the fief list: Jac, La Marche, Marroux, Tumirdus, Corraise,
  Arrha, Rochejaquelein, Chatou, Everlon, Bicêtre, Barielle, Karsten, Rougeamera, Brillagne, and the
  abbreviations DR, HE, RH, MB, WE, PC. Buagott (Oscarl) wasn't found on the map.
- Abbreviations shared by two fiefs (FA: Faste / Falaise, RE: Rempart / Reliette) count for both.
